      KNOW THE MODEL

      A focused engineering tool.

      Ideal DC sources and resistors. LEDs use a forward threshold plus 10 Ω dynamic resistance; closed switches use 0.01 Ω. Tiny grounding conductance stabilizes floating nodes.

      No transients, temperature effects, device tolerances, or reverse breakdown. The agent edits component values, polarity, and enabled state; it cannot rewire nodes or change the targets.
